Breathmarks is a beta-reading desk for fiction. A friend reads your draft silently on their laptop. The Presage SmartSpectra SDK reads their breathing, pulse and blinks from the camera, paragraph by paragraph, against their own calm 30 seconds. They see the marks first, keep what they like, and only then share them with you.

Layout

What the camera is used for

While you read, the SmartSpectra SDK estimates your breathing (rate and chest movement), pulse rate and blinks. Each paragraph is compared with your own calm 30 seconds before reading. Nothing is compared with other people.

What stays with you

No video or images are recorded or saved. Readings stay in this app’s memory. The camera turns off as soon as you finish. You see every mark first, can remove any of them, or delete everything.

What the writer gets

Only if you press Share with the writer: the text with the marks you kept and a short summary. Never video, raw pulse or breathing data, or timings. These are camera estimates for feedback, not medical data, and they do not read emotions.

Network and SDK details

The SDK needs the internet to check the Presage API key and meter usage. SDK telemetry is off unless you turn it on in Settings. Presage documents that when breathing and pulse are both active the SDK may send pulse and breathing numbers (never video) to its insights service; Breathmarks never requests or shows insights. To avoid this, turn off Pulse rate in Settings. See Presage’s Telemetry & Privacy and Privacy Policy.

Requested signals: breathing rate and chest/abdomen traces, pulse rate, blinking, talking (only to ignore breathing while you speak). Not requested: expressions, face landmarks, HRV.
